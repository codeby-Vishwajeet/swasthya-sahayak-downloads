## 1. Purpose and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator identified above (referred to as **“we,” “us,”** or **“the App operator”**) may collect, use, store, and share information when you use the Swasthya Sahayak Android application (the **“App”**).

The App provides health-awareness and preliminary screening features. Some information submitted to the App may reveal a person's health or well-being and should therefore be treated as sensitive information.

This Policy applies to the official Android application and the services it uses. It does not automatically cover unrelated websites, third-party apps, or services that have their own privacy notices.

## 2. Information We May Process

The exact information processed depends on the features you use and the configuration of the released version. It may include the following categories:

### A. Account and profile information

- name, email address, and account identifier used for sign-in;
- profile details you choose to provide; and
- a profile photograph, if profile-photo upload is enabled and you choose to add one.

### B. Health and screening information

- answers you enter in health-screening questionnaires;
- symptoms, health concerns, age or age-range information, lifestyle information, and other details you voluntarily provide in a screening flow;
- screening summaries, risk indicators, recommendations, and reports generated from your answers; and
- information you voluntarily include when asking for health-related guidance.

Please do not submit information that is not requested or reasonably necessary for the feature you are using.

### C. Reports submitted for review

If the report-submission feature is enabled, information included in a report may be stored in the App's configured database or review workflow when you choose to submit it. The exact recipient, access permissions, and review process must be explained in the App before submission. A stored report is not necessarily a report that has been received or reviewed by a doctor.

### D. Technical and device information

Depending on the services enabled, technical information may be processed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the App. This may include app version, Android/device details, sign-in events, server logs, error information, and a push-notification token if notifications are enabled.

### E. Permissions and uploaded files

The App should request device permissions only when needed for a feature. If photo or camera access is enabled for profile-photo upload, the selected image or captured photo may be processed for that purpose. The App should not access photos or the camera merely because this Policy exists; actual permissions depend on the installed build and Android permission prompts.

## 3. How We Use Information

Subject to applicable law and the choices presented in the App, information may be used to:

- create, authenticate, and manage your account;
- provide the screening, health-awareness, report, and profile features you request;
- generate a screening summary or AI-assisted response;
- save information or reports you choose to save or submit;
- deliver notifications where you have enabled them;
- maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the App;
- respond to support, privacy, or safety requests; and
- comply with legal obligations or protect users and the App from fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

We should collect and process only information that is reasonably needed for these purposes and should not reuse health information for an unrelated purpose without an appropriate lawful basis and any consent required by law.

## 4. AI Features and External AI Providers

When you use an AI-assisted feature, the text, questionnaire answers, or other information needed to respond to that request may be sent to the AI service provider configured for that feature. The App's current or intended integrations may include **Groq** and **Sarvam**. The exact provider depends on the feature and the version of the App.

AI providers process requests under their own service terms and privacy practices. Their processing, storage, and retention settings may change. We do not promise that an AI provider stores data only in India or that a request is never retained by that provider. Before release, the App operator must verify the provider, the data sent, the provider's applicable terms, and the available data-retention settings.

Do not include your full name, exact address, phone number, government identity number, or another direct identifier in an AI prompt unless the App clearly requires it and explains why. Where technically possible, the App should send only the minimum information needed to generate the requested response.

AI-generated content may be inaccurate or incomplete and is not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional.
